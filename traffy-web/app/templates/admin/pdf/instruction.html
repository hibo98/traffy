Internet in the Student Dormitory {{ community }}

The dormitory network is part of the university network.

Therefore, there is a data limit for all users.

Your initial volume is: {{ initial_volume }} GiB

Savings: You receive {{ daily_topup_volume }} GiB every day. Data usage is only counted between 6 PM and 8 AM; during the day (8 AM – 6 PM) usage is not counted. Unused data is carried over to the next day, up to a maximum of {{ max_saved_volume}} GiB.

When your data is used up: Your internet speed will be reduced to {{ shaping_speed }}/s. Only university services (e.g. learning platforms) will remain at full speed. With the next daily credit of {{ daily_topup_volume}} GiB, the speed limit will be lifted automatically.
